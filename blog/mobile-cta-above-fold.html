Put one mobile CTA above the fold

Tip · Spiel Ventures · republished for inbound SEO

On a phone, the first screen should answer: what you do, where you are, and how to reach you. One primary button — Call or Get a quote — beats three vague links.

Checklist: sticky header tap-to-call · short form under the offer · no modal before first tap.
