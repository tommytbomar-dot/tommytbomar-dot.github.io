Chiropractic clinic local SEO & new-patient path

Chiropractors win on trust and easy booking. This page covers the listing, page and intake details that most often block a new patient from calling.

Remote and async. No guarantees on rankings or call volume. No invented case studies — see the honest case-study shell.

What new patients check before they call

  • Who you treat and how. A plain list (low back pain, neck pain, sports injury, auto-accident care) beats vague 'wellness' copy. Say what a first visit includes and how long it takes.
  • Doctor credentials and licensing. Show the DC credential, state licensure and years in practice — truthfully. Link to the Texas Board of Chiropractic Examiners lookup if you wish.
  • Insurance and cash pricing. 'Do you take my insurance?' and 'what's a visit cost without insurance?' are top questions. A short table of accepted plans and a new-patient cash price saves phone time.
  • Auto-accident intake. If you see accident cases, give a separate short page and form; these patients are often hurt and on a phone.
  • Online booking that works on a phone. Test your scheduling link end-to-end, in an incognito tab, on cellular data.

Compliance reminders (not legal advice)

  • Health-claim wording: avoid promising cures or guaranteed outcomes. Check your state board's advertising rules and the FTC's guidance on health claims.
  • Patient testimonials: only post with written permission, and never share protected health information in a public review reply. Reply generically: 'We can't discuss care details here — please call us.'
  • Do not put health details in a plain-email contact form unless your tools are set up for it. Keep the web form to name, phone, preferred time, and 'reason for visit' in general terms.

Five-step free fix list

  1. Make the booking button the first thing on mobile and test it today.
  2. List accepted insurance plans in text on one page.
  3. Add a first-visit page: what happens, how long, what to wear, what it costs.
  4. Reply to your last five Google reviews without sharing any health detail.
  5. Check that your GBP primary category is Chiropractor and hours match the door.
