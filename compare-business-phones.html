Texting rules you cannot skip

  • US business texting from a local number generally requires carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) - your provider will walk you through it.
  • Get consent before sending marketing texts. Replying to a customer who texted you first is different from texting a list.
  • Always honor STOP.

My review reply setup and missed-call scripts follow these rules. This is not legal advice.

My take

If you take fewer than 20 calls a week, a free number that forwards to your cell plus a good voicemail greeting is enough to start. Upgrade when you start losing calls or sharing the inbox.
