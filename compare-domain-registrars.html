Domain registrar comparison for a local business: Cloudflare, Namecheap, GoDaddy, Porkbun

The domain is the one thing you should own outright. Here is what matters when you pick where to register it.

Disclosure: I am not enrolled in any affiliate program for the products on this page, and none of the links below carry an affiliate ID. If that changes, I will mark each affiliate link as such on this page. Prices and limits change; check each vendor's own page before you buy. Last reviewed October 2026.

What matters

  • Renewal price - first-year promos hide higher renewals. Compare renewal, not year one.
  • WHOIS privacy - free at most registrars now; confirm.
  • Transfer lock and 2FA - turn on both.
  • Your name as registrant - never let a web designer register it in their own name.

My take

For most East Texas trades businesses, the cheapest safe setup is: register at a registrar with fair renewals, use your own email, turn on auto-renew and 2FA, and keep login details in a password manager you control. If a vendor already holds your domain, ask for the auth code and registrant contact - you are entitled to it.
