What I use

This site runs on GitHub Pages because it is plain HTML and I want zero cost and zero maintenance. For a local business that wants a contact form without a third-party form tool, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages with a form handler is the usual step up.

Pick quickly

  • One-page brochure, you are comfortable with GitHub: GitHub Pages
  • Want the fastest CDN and room to grow: Cloudflare Pages
  • Want a form with no code: Netlify (watch the free submission cap)
  • Business site on Vercel: budget for a paid plan or use another host
