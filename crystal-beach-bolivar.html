What Bolivar Peninsula businesses usually need

  • A real service-area statement. The Peninsula is long and narrow. Contractors, cleaners and delivery businesses should say which stretch they cover (Port Bolivar, Crystal Beach, Gilchrist, High Island) rather than just "Bolivar".
  • Ferry and road reality. Many customers arrive via the Galveston–Bolivar ferry or from the east on Hwy 124/87. Say how people reach you and what to expect on busy weekends.
  • Rental-host essentials. Check-in steps, trash day, beach-access rules and quiet hours belong in a welcome guide, not buried in texts. Our STR templates cover this.
  • Seasonal hours. Many Peninsula businesses cut winter hours. Update GBP the same day you change the door sign.
  • Spotty signal. Keep pages light and forms short; heavy sliders and auto-playing video hurt most when bars are low.
  • Storm-recovery trust. Post licensing, insurance and a plain scope description — buyers vet contractors carefully after storms.

Free fix list — do these this week (no purchase needed)

  1. Write one sentence naming exactly which parts of the Peninsula you serve and put it on your homepage and GBP description.
  2. Add the ferry/road directions line to your contact page.
  3. Check that your phone number is identical on site, GBP, Facebook and one directory.
  4. Test your homepage on throttled mobile data (Chrome DevTools > Network > Slow 4G) and note anything over 4 seconds to show content.
  5. Add a winter-hours entry to GBP special hours.
