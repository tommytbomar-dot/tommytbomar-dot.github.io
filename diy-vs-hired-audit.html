The DIY route (free)

  • Run PageSpeed Insights on your homepage and one service page; write down mobile LCP and the top two opportunities.
  • Walk through your site on a real phone: can you tap-to-call in one move? Can you submit the form with one thumb?
  • Compare name, address, phone and hours on your site, GBP and one directory.
  • Count form fields. Long forms without a clear reason lose leads.
  • Use the free mobile lead form checklist and 7-day lead checklist.

Best when: you have an hour, are comfortable editing your site and will ship fixes this week.

When paying makes sense

  • You have done the checks twice and still don't know what to fix first.
  • You are about to spend money on ads or a redesign and want the leak found first.
  • You want a dated document to hand to a web person or an employee.
  • You are too close to the site to see friction a stranger sees in seconds.
