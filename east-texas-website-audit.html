East Texas website audit

Local service businesses around East Texas and the Gulf Coast usually don’t need a redesign brochure — they need the mobile path from search → trust → call/form to work.

What a useful audit actually checks

  1. Mobile CTA path — can a stranger request help in under a minute on a phone?
  2. NAP + hours — site, Google Business, and Facebook don’t contradict each other.
  3. Form friction — fields, keyboards, thank-you / notification path.
  4. Speed & tap targets — bloated builders and tiny buttons that miss thumbs.
  5. Local trust cues — real service area language, not generic stock-city copy.
