Form CTA not converting

Traffic is fine. Clicks look okay. Leads are flat. Usually the CTA path on mobile is broken — not “you need more content.”

Symptoms worth trusting

  • High mobile bounce on contact / booking pages
  • Form starts but few completions
  • Calls happen from Google Maps, almost never from your site button
  • “Submit” does nothing or shows a generic error on some phones

Fixes that usually move the needle first

  1. One primary action. Call OR book OR message — not three equal buttons fighting for attention.
  2. Put the CTA where thumbs land. Sticky bottom bar or mid-page after a short benefit line beats a footer-only form.
  3. Promise the next step. “We’ll text you within 2 hours” beats silence after submit.
  4. Cut fields. Every optional field you keep as required kills completion rate.
  5. Test the fail path. Wrong CAPTCHA, broken webhook, and “success” pages that 404 kill quiet leads.

Quick self-test (5 minutes)

  • Open your site on your phone on cellular (not office Wi‑Fi).
  • From the homepage, time how long until you can request help with one thumb.
  • Submit a test lead with a unique phrase in the message field — confirm you actually receive it.
