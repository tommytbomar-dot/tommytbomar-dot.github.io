What Galveston businesses usually need

  • Hours that match reality in every season. Island traffic swings with summer weekends, cruise-ship days and events such as Mardi Gras! Galveston, Dickens on the Strand and the Lone Star Rally. Set special hours in Google Business Profile ahead of each one.
  • Phone-first pages. A visitor on the Seawall is on a phone, in sun glare, with one thumb. Tap-to-call and a one-field quote or booking form beat a long contact page.
  • Weather-closure notice. Hurricane season runs June 1–Nov 30. Keep a one-line site banner and GBP temporary-closure process ready so you are not editing five places during a storm watch.
  • Parking and arrival details. "Where do I park / where do I check in?" is the top pre-visit question for tours, rentals and restaurants. Put the answer above the fold.
  • NAP consistency. Island businesses often rebrand or change owners; stale name/address/phone listings on old directories are a common source of wrong-number calls.
  • Short-term rental hosts: check the City of Galveston's current STR registration and tax rules, then make your listing headline and welcome info match what you actually offer.

Free fix list — do these this week (no purchase needed)

  1. Open your Google Business Profile on a phone and confirm hours, phone and website link are right today.
  2. Add special hours for the next holiday or festival weekend.
  3. Put your phone number as a tap-to-call link in the top 20% of your homepage on mobile.
  4. Cut your quote/booking form to name, phone, one question.
  5. Search your business name + 'Galveston' and note any listing with a different phone or address.
