Condensate Drain Line

East Texas HVAC & trades glossary · plain English for homeowners and contractors.

Glossary All 10 terms Definitions and general guidance only — confirm code and rebate rules locally.

Definition

An air conditioner removes moisture from indoor air. That water drips into a pan and leaves through the condensate drain line. When the line clogs with algae and sludge, water backs up, trips a safety switch and can leak into the ceiling or closet.

In plain English

Many systems have a float switch that shuts the AC off when the pan fills. If your AC stopped on a humid day and the pan is full, a clogged drain is a likely cause.

Prevention: annual or twice-yearly flushing, a clean filter, and a properly sloped line. Ask a technician which cleaning method suits your system.

Attic and closet air handlers cause the most damage because water reaches drywall before anyone notices. A secondary pan with its own switch is cheap insurance.

A repeat clog can mean wrong slope, a missing trap or a disconnected line.

Common mistakes

  • Pouring harsh chemicals without knowing the pipe or coil material.
  • Ignoring a tripped float switch by bypassing it.
  • Quoting a full 'AC repair' without checking the drain first.

FAQ

Why is my AC leaking water inside?
Often a clogged condensate drain, a cracked pan or a frozen coil melting. A technician can tell which.

How often should it be cleaned?
Commonly once or twice a season in humid climates. Your technician can advise.
