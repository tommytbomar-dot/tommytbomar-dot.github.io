East Texas HVAC & trades glossary

10 plain-English terms for homeowners and contractors. Written to be linked from service pages and Google Business Profile posts.

Definitions are general guidance, not engineering advice. Plumbing and electrical terms are on the roadmap.

Terms

  • SEER2 — Current efficiency rating for central AC and heat pumps (since 2023 test rules).
  • Tonnage (HVAC) — How much cooling capacity a system has; 1 ton = 12,000 BTU/hour.
  • Manual J Load Calculation — The industry method for sizing heating and cooling equipment.
  • Heat Pump — One system that cools in summer and heats in winter by moving heat.
  • Ductwork / Duct Sealing — The hidden tubes that carry conditioned air — and where much of it leaks out.
  • Refrigerant (R-410A / R-454B) — The fluid that carries heat in your AC; the standard is changing.
  • AFUE (Furnace Efficiency) — How much of a gas furnace's fuel becomes heat for your home.
  • Emergency HVAC / No-Cool Call — Urgent same-day or after-hours repair for no-cool and no-heat.
  • Condensate Drain Line — The pipe that carries AC water out of the house; clogs shut systems down.
  • Smart Thermostat / Controls — Wi-Fi thermostats and zoning controls — useful when installed correctly.
