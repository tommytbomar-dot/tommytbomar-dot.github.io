Google Business Profile getting no calls

Views without calls usually means trust or friction — not “you need more posts.” Work this list before you pay for ads.

Check these first

  • Primary phone is the number you actually answer (or forwards cleanly).
  • Hours are correct including holidays — wrong hours kill “Call now” confidence.
  • Categories match what people search (too-broad categories bury you).
  • Address / service area settings match how you really serve customers.
  • Photos show the real place or crew — empty profiles look abandoned.
  • Website link lands on a mobile page with a clear Call / Form CTA (not a PDF or dead builder page).

Website side of the problem

Many profiles look fine. The linked site then loses the lead: tiny Call button, form that fails on iPhone, or NAP that doesn’t match the profile. Fix the site path or the Map Pack click is wasted.

Reviews — keep it honest

  • Ask happy customers for a short, real review. Don’t buy reviews or invent stories.
  • Reply to new reviews — even brief replies show you’re active.
