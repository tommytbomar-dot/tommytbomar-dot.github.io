What Houston Heights and Montrose businesses usually need

  • Walk-in and appointment clarity. Say plainly whether you take walk-ins, and show hours that include holiday and event variations.
  • Neighborhood relevance. Mention the streets and landmarks locals actually use (19th Street, Heights Blvd, Westheimer, Montrose Blvd) on the page where it is natural, not in keyword lists.
  • Parking and transit info. Street parking, a lot, a nearby METRO stop — one line saves a lot of calls.
  • Dense competition, so photos and menus matter. Use your own current photos and keep the price/menu readable on a phone.
  • Review replies in a human voice. Short, specific replies read better than copy-paste thank-yous. The review-reply bank pack covers this.
  • Contractors serving Houston-wide. Use a clear service-area list instead of a single neighborhood claim.
