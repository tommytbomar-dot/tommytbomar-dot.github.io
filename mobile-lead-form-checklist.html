Mobile lead form checklist

If most of your traffic is on phones, your form is the phone call you never took. Use this checklist before you spend on more ads.

1. Above-the-fold CTA path

  • Can a visitor see a clear next step (Call / Book / Message) without scrolling on a typical phone screen?
  • Is tap target at least ~44px tall with real thumb space (not a tiny text link)?
  • Does the primary button say what happens next (“Request a quote”, not “Submit”)?

2. Form friction

  • Keep fields to what you actually use: name, phone or email, one optional note.
  • Disable “required” on anything you never follow up on.
  • Use the correct keyboard: type="tel" for phone, type="email" for email.
  • Test on a real phone — auto-zoom on iOS often means font-size under 16px on inputs.

3. NAP + hours consistency

  • Name, address, phone match your Google Business Profile and footer.
  • Hours visible near the CTA (people abandon forms when unsure you’re open).
  • Click-to-call tel: link works on Android and iPhone.

4. After submit

  • Thank-you page or message confirms receipt and sets expectation (e.g. “We reply same business day”).
  • You get notified by email/SMS — not only a dashboard you forget to open.
  • Spam filtering isn’t silently dropping genuine leads.
