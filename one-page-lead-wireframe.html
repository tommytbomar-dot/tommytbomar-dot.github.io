One-page lead wireframe (local services)

You don’t need a 12-page brochure site to get phone calls. One clear page often outperforms a bloated builder theme.

Suggested order (top → bottom)

  1. Hero — who you help + where + primary CTA (Call / Request quote).
  2. Three proof points — years, service area, one concrete promise (no invented awards).
  3. Services short list — 4–6 items max people actually book.
  4. Form or sticky call — low friction; thank-you expectation set.
  5. NAP + hours + map — match Google Business exactly.
  6. FAQ — 3 questions that unblock booking (pricing style, service area, response time).

What to cut

  • Auto-playing video above the fold on mobile
  • Duplicate menus that push the CTA below the first screen
  • Stock “Our Team” blocks with no real photos yet
