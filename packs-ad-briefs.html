### Brief 1 — HVAC
- **Goal of the post:** get a direct call or booking request.
- **Headline (≤7 words):** AC not cooling in the heat — or your own wording in the customer's language.
- **Subline (≤12 words):** [City]-based, [years] years in business, licensed [license #] — only true facts.
- **Action line:** Tap-to-call: [phone]
- **Offer:** Same-day service request
- **Photo to shoot:** a close photo of your own tech at a condenser, or your truck at a customer's home (with permission).
- **Overlay:** black 50%; text white; action line in your brand color.
- **Caption (copy/paste, edit):**
  > AC not cooling in the heat? We're [Business], serving [City]. Same-day service request. Tap-to-call: [phone]. Call or message — we answer [hours]. 
  > Licensed #[license]. Service area: [towns].
- **Check before posting:** after-hours answer promise (only if true).
- **Alt text for accessibility:** "[Describe photo]. Text reads: [headline]. [action line]."
- **Variants to test (change one thing):** (a) different headline, (b) different photo, (c) action line with vs without phone.
- **How to judge it (no paid tools):** ask new callers "how did you find us?" and write it down; check GBP Insights calls/clicks the week after.
