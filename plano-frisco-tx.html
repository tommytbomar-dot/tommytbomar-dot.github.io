What Plano and Frisco businesses usually need

  • Heavy competition, so be specific. "Plano plumber" has dozens of near-identical pages. Name neighborhoods, response times you can actually meet, and license numbers.
  • Multi-location NAP. If you serve both Plano and Frisco, decide which address is the GBP location and what the service-area list says; do not create duplicate listings for one office.
  • Appointment friction. Busy suburban households book on phones in the evening. Show open appointment windows or a clear "we call back within X" promise you can keep.
  • Review velocity and replies. A steady trickle of recent, answered reviews matters more than a big old total. Use a simple ask script after each job.
  • Page speed on shared themes. Many suburban sites run heavy page-builder themes; compress hero images and remove unused plugins first.
  • Pay attention to forms. Over 5–7 fields without a progress cue usually costs leads; ask for the rest on the callback.

Free fix list — do these this week (no purchase needed)

  1. Run PageSpeed Insights on your homepage's mobile tab; write down LCP and the top two opportunities.
  2. List every place your name, address, phone appear and make them identical.
  3. Add your license number and years in business (true ones) near the call button.
  4. Answer every unanswered Google review from the past 90 days.
  5. Shorten your main form to name, phone, service needed.
