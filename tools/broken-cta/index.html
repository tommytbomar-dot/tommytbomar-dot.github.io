Broken CTA Detector

A bookmarklet that scans the page you are on for call-to-action buttons and links that are dead, empty, disabled, mistyped (tel:/mailto:), too small to tap, or missing from the first screen. It runs only in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Step 1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

CTA Check

Step 2. Open any website (yours or a prospect's) and click the bookmark.

Some sites block bookmarklets with a Content Security Policy; the detector will not run there. These are basic heuristics: they catch common breakage, not everything. Test your CTAs by hand on a real phone.
