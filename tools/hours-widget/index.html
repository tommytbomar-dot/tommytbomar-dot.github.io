Open / Closed Hours Widget

A tiny script (about 3 KB, no dependencies, no tracking) that shows Open now · until 5 PM or Closed · opens tomorrow at 9 AM, calculated in your business's time zone.

Live demo

(sample hours: Mon-Fri 8-5, Sat 9-12, Central time)

Install



  • data-hours: JSON; keys mon…sun, each a list of "HH:MM-HH:MM" (24h). Omit a day or use [] for closed. An end earlier than the start means overnight.
  • data-tz: an IANA time zone, e.g. America/Chicago.
  • data-unstyled: add it to skip the built-in pill style and style the element yourself (it carries data-open="true|false").


Keep widget hours identical to your Google Business Profile hours. Holiday closures are not handled: edit the JSON. The widget runs on the visitor's clock but displays in your zone.
